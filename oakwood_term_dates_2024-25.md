## Important Notes

1. **Early finish on last day of autumn term:** School finishes at 1:30pm on Friday 20th December 2024 (not the usual 3:15pm). Please make arrangements for earlier collection.

2. **INSET days:** School is closed to pupils on INSET days. These are staff training days.

3. **Wraparound care:** Breakfast Club and After-School Club run as normal during term time but are **NOT available on INSET days**.

4. **Holiday requests:** The school strongly discourages term-time holidays. Leave of absence will only be granted in exceptional circumstances. Fines may be issued by Devon County Council for unauthorised absence.

5. **Planning ahead:** Please avoid booking family holidays or appointments during term time where possible. Good attendance is crucial for your child's education.
