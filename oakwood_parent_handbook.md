## Attendance and Absence

### Reporting Absence

If your child is unable to attend school, you must notify us before 9:00am on each day of absence.

**Methods to report absence:**
- Telephone: 01392 555 0123 (press 1 for absence line)
- Email: attendance@oakwood.sch.uk

Please provide:
- Your child's full name and class
- Reason for absence
- Expected return date

### Authorised vs Unauthorised Absence

**Authorised absences** include:
- Illness
- Medical/dental appointments (please try to book outside school hours where possible)
- Religious observance
- Exceptional family circumstances (approved in advance)

**Unauthorised absences** include:
- Holidays during term time
- Shopping trips or family days out
- Looking after siblings or other family members
- Birthdays

### Holiday Requests

The Department for Education has made it clear that headteachers may not grant any leave of absence during term time unless there are exceptional circumstances. 

We strongly discourage term-time holidays. Fines may be issued by the Local Authority for unauthorised absence.

If you feel you have exceptional circumstances, please complete a Leave of Absence request form (available from the school office) at least 4 weeks in advance.

## Illness and Medical Matters

### When to Keep Your Child at Home

Please do not send your child to school if they:
- Have a temperature of 38°C or above
- Have been sick or had diarrhoea in the last 48 hours
- Have an infectious disease (see list below)
- Are unwell enough that they cannot participate in normal school activities

### 48-Hour Rule

**If your child has been sick or had diarrhoea, they must stay at home for 48 hours after the last episode.** This helps prevent the spread of infection to other children and staff.

### Infectious Diseases

Some illnesses require exclusion from school for specific periods:

| Illness | Exclusion Period |
|---------|------------------|
| Chickenpox | Until all blisters have crusted over (usually 5 days) |
| Impetigo | Until lesions are crusted and healed, or 48 hours after starting antibiotics |
| Scarlet Fever | 24 hours after starting antibiotics |
| Slapped Cheek (Fifth Disease) | None (once rash appears, no longer infectious) |
| Hand, Foot and Mouth | Until feeling better and temperature has resolved |
| Conjunctivitis | None, but see GP if severe |

**Head Lice:** Children with head lice do not need to be excluded from school. Please treat at home and notify the school office so we can alert other parents (anonymously).

### Medication at School

We can only administer prescribed medication that needs to be taken during the school day. You must complete a **Medication Consent Form** (available from the office) before any medication can be given.

All medication must:
- Be in its original container with the prescription label
- Be handed to the office staff (not sent in book bags)
- Include clear dosage instructions

**We cannot administer paracetamol or other non-prescribed medication** unless there is a specific care plan in place (e.g., for chronic conditions).

### Asthma Inhalers

Children with asthma should have an inhaler in school at all times. Please ensure:
- The inhaler is clearly labelled with your child's name
- The expiry date is checked regularly
- A spare inhaler is available if needed
- The school office has a copy of your child's asthma care plan

## School Meals and Break Time Snacks

### Hot School Meals

Hot meals are provided daily and must be ordered via **ParentPay** by **Wednesday each week** for the following week.

The cost is **£2.50 per meal** (KS2 only - Reception, Year 1 and Year 2 receive free school meals under the Universal Infant Free School Meals scheme).

A menu is sent home at the start of each half-term and is available on our website.

### Free School Meals (Income-Based)

If you receive certain benefits, your child may be eligible for free school meals regardless of year group. This eligibility also brings additional funding to the school (Pupil Premium), so please apply even if your child is in Reception, Year 1, or Year 2.

To check eligibility and apply, visit: www.devon.gov.uk/freeschoolmeals

### Packed Lunches

If your child brings a packed lunch, please ensure it is healthy and balanced. 

**Please do not include:**
- Nuts or products containing nuts (we have children with severe allergies)
- Glass bottles
- Fizzy drinks or energy drinks
- Excessive sweets or chocolate bars

### Break Time Snacks

**Morning break** (KS2 only - KS1 and Reception receive free fruit):
- Children may bring a healthy snack
- Suitable snacks: fruit, vegetables, breadsticks, cheese
- Not permitted: crisps, chocolate bars, sweets

**Water bottles:**
Children should bring a named water bottle to school each day. These can be refilled throughout the day. Water is the only drink permitted in classrooms.
