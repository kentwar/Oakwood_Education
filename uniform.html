School Uniform Policy

We believe that school uniform helps create a sense of identity and belonging for our pupils. Our uniform is practical, affordable, and available from multiple suppliers.

Standard Uniform

Boys and Girls

  • Green sweatshirt or cardigan with school logo
  • White or green polo shirt (with or without logo)
  • Grey or black trousers, shorts, skirt, or pinafore
  • Green and white checked summer dress (optional for summer term)
  • Black shoes (not trainers)
  • White, grey, or green socks or tights

PE Kit

  • Plain white t-shirt
  • Black or green shorts
  • Black or green jogging bottoms (for outdoor PE in winter)
  • Trainers or plimsolls

PE Days:

  • Reception, Year 1, Year 2: Monday and Thursday
  • Year 3, Year 4: Tuesday and Friday
  • Year 5, Year 6: Wednesday and Friday

Children should come to school in their PE kit on PE days.

Outdoor Learning

All children will need:

  • Waterproof coat (kept in school)
  • Wellington boots (kept in school for outdoor learning sessions)

Jewellery and Hair

  • Stud earrings only (no hoops or dangly earrings)
  • No other jewellery except watches
  • Long hair should be tied back for safety and hygiene
  • Extreme hairstyles or hair colours are not permitted

Where to Buy

School uniform with logos can be purchased from:

  • Uniform4Kids: www.uniform4kids.com
  • School Office: Limited stock of second-hand uniform available

Plain uniform items (polo shirts, trousers, skirts) can be purchased from any retailer.

Financial Support: If you need help with uniform costs, please contact the school office in confidence. We have a uniform exchange scheme and may be able to offer additional support.
