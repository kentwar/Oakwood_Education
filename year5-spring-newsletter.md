## Our Topic: Space - The Final Frontier

This term we're launching into space! From our solar system to distant galaxies, we'll be exploring the wonders of the universe.

### What we'll be learning:

**Science - Earth and Space**
- The solar system and planets
- Day and night - Earth's rotation
- The Moon and its phases
- Famous space missions and astronauts
- Is there life on other planets?

**English**
- Science fiction stories
- Persuasive writing (design your own planet and convince others to visit!)
- Non-fiction reports about space exploration
- Poetry inspired by the night sky
- Debate: Should we spend money on space exploration?

**Maths**
- Large numbers (thinking about distances in space)
- Decimals and percentages
- Area and perimeter
- Problem-solving using space-themed contexts
- Statistics and graphs (comparing planets)

**Computing**
- Programming rockets using Scratch
- Creating a space presentation
- Digital research skills

**Art & DT**
- Perspective drawing (spacescapes)
- Creating planetary models
- Designing and building rockets (linked to forces)

## Homework

Homework is set via **Google Classroom every Monday** and should be completed by the following Monday.

**Weekly homework:**
- **Reading:** 15-20 minutes daily (reading record signed by parents)
- **Spelling:** Learn weekly spellings (test every Friday)
- **Maths:** MyMaths online activities or practice paper
- **Topic project:** One space-related project per half term (presentation, model, research report - your choice!)

## Times Tables

By the end of Year 5, children should know all times tables up to 12x12 fluently. We'll be having weekly times table tests on Fridays.

Please practise regularly using:
- Times Tables Rock Stars (online)
- Quick-fire questions at home
- Times table apps
