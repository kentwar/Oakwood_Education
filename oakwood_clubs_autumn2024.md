## Important Information

### How to Book
All bookings must be made through **ParentPay**. Log in to your account and select "Clubs & Activities" from the menu.

Bookings open: **Monday 2nd September at 6:00pm**  
Bookings close: **Friday 6th September at 4:00pm**

Places are allocated on a **first-come, first-served basis**, so please book early to avoid disappointment!

### Start Date
All clubs will begin in the **week commencing Monday 16th September 2024**.

### Half-Term Break
There will be **no clubs during the week of 28th October - 1st November** (half-term week).

### Payment
- Paid clubs (£3 per session) are charged at the start of each half-term
- Autumn first half-term (Sept 16 - Oct 25): 6 weeks = £18 per club
- Autumn second half-term (Nov 4 - Dec 20): 7 weeks = £21 per club
- **Refunds are only available for absence due to illness** (with a doctor's note)

### Collection
- After-school clubs finish at 4:15pm or 4:30pm as stated above
- Please collect your child promptly from the main school entrance
- Children must be signed out by an adult
- If you will be late, please call the school office

### Commitment
We ask that children commit to attending for at least half a term. This helps clubs run smoothly and ensures all children get the most benefit from the activities.

### Cancellations
If we need to cancel a club due to staff illness or other unforeseen circumstances, we will notify you via text message by 2:00pm on the day. Credits will be applied to your ParentPay account.

### Waiting Lists
If a club is full, you can join a waiting list. We will contact you if a space becomes available.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can my child attend multiple clubs?**  
Yes! Children can attend as many clubs as they wish (and you can afford!). Please just ensure they can manage their homework alongside club commitments.

**What if my child wants to try a club before committing?**  
Unfortunately, we cannot offer trial sessions as spaces are limited. However, if your child attends once and decides it's not for them, please let us know and we'll process a refund for remaining sessions.

**Can Reception children attend clubs?**  
Not during autumn term, we're sorry. Reception children need time to settle into school routines first. We'll open some clubs to Reception in the spring and summer terms.

**What happens if my child is on the waiting list?**  
We'll contact you via email if a space becomes available. You'll have 48 hours to accept the place before we offer it to the next person on the list.

**My child has additional needs - can they still attend?**  
Absolutely! Please contact the club leader or Mr. Chen (SENCO) to discuss any adjustments or support your child might need. We want all children to be able to participate.
