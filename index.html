Welcome to Oakwood Primary

Oakwood Primary School is a vibrant community primary school serving children aged 4-11 in the heart of our local community.

School Hours

School starts at 8:45am and finishes at 3:15pm.

Doors open at 8:35am for children to come into class.

Our School

We are a one-form entry primary school with approximately 210 pupils across seven classes from Reception to Year 6.
