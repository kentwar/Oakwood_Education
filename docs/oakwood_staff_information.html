How to Contact Staff

General enquiries: Please contact the school office first on 01392 555 0123 or office@oakwood.sch.uk

Class teachers: The best way to contact your child's class teacher is via email (addresses listed above). Teachers typically respond within 48 hours during term time.

Urgent matters: If you need to speak to someone urgently during the school day, please call the office and we will arrange for someone to call you back.

End of day messages: If you need to send a message to your child's teacher about collection arrangements, please call or email the office before 2:30pm.

Safeguarding
