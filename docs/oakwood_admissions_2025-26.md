## How to Apply

### Step 1: Check Eligibility
Your child must be 4 years old by 31st August 2025 to start Reception in September 2025.

Children born between:
- 1st September 2020 - 31st August 2021: Start Reception September 2025

### Step 2: Apply Online
**Applications must be made through Devon County Council**, not directly to the school.

Apply online at: **www.devon.gov.uk/admissions**

**Application period:** Opens 9th September 2024  
**Deadline:** 15th January 2025 (midnight)

⚠️ **Late applications** will be processed after all on-time applications and are much less likely to receive an offer from your preferred school.

### Step 3: List Your Preferences
You can list up to three schools in order of preference. You should use all three preferences to maximize your chances of getting a school place.

**We recommend:**
1. Your preferred school (e.g., Oakwood)
2. Another school you would be happy with
3. Your catchment area school (if different) as a safety option

### Step 4: Wait for Offer Day
All applicants will receive an email on **16th April 2025** with their school allocation.

### Step 5: Accept or Decline
You have until **30th April 2025** to accept or decline your place.

## Admissions Criteria

If Oakwood receives more applications than we have places available, places are allocated using the following criteria (in order):

### 1. Children in Care and Previously in Care
Children who are looked after or were previously looked after (including children adopted from care).

### 2. Siblings
Children who have a sibling at the school at the time of admission.

**Definition of sibling:** 
- Brother or sister
- Half-brother or half-sister
- Step-brother or step-sister
- Adopted brother or sister
- Living at the same address

**Note:** Siblings in Year 6 who will leave before September 2025 do NOT count.

### 3. Medical or Social Grounds
Children for whom there are exceptional medical or social reasons for attending the school (supported by professional evidence).

### 4. Distance
Children living nearest to the school, measured by the shortest walking route using public roads and footpaths.

## Starting School

### Can My Child Start Part-Time?

All children are entitled to attend full-time from the beginning of the school year. However, we understand some children may benefit from a gradual introduction.

**Our approach:**
- **First week (Sept 3-5):** All children attend mornings only (8:50am - 12:00pm)
- **Second week onwards (from Sept 8):** Full days for all children

If you feel your child needs a more gradual start, please discuss this with the Headteacher.

### Can I Defer Entry?

If your child's 5th birthday is between September and March, you may defer entry until later in the academic year, or request part-time attendance, but **they must start by the term after they turn 5**.

**Summer-born children** (born April-August): You may request delayed entry to Reception the following year. This is not automatic and must be discussed with the school and Local Authority.

For more information, contact the admissions team at: educationandfamilies@devon.gov.uk

## In-Year Admissions (Moving to the Area)

If you need a school place during the school year (not September Reception intake), the process is different:

### For Year Groups with Available Spaces:
1. Apply via Devon County Council: www.devon.gov.uk/admissions
2. The school will be asked if we have space
3. If space available, you'll be offered a place
4. Start date agreed with the school

### For Year Groups That Are Full:
1. Apply via Devon County Council
2. Your child will be added to the waiting list
3. If a space becomes available, it will be offered to the child highest on the waiting list (using the same criteria as initial admissions)

**Advice:** If moving to the area, apply as early as possible to maximize chances of getting your preferred school.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I have a guaranteed place because I live in catchment?**  
No. Living in catchment does not guarantee a place. You must still apply and will be considered alongside all other applicants using our admissions criteria.

**What if I don't get my first choice?**  
You will be allocated one of your preferences if possible. If none of your preferences can offer a place, Devon County Council will allocate the nearest school with spaces available. You can join waiting lists for your preferred schools.

**Can I appeal if my child doesn't get a place?**  
Yes. You have the right to appeal. Appeal forms and information will be sent with your allocation letter if unsuccessful.

**When should I apply if we're moving house?**  
Apply as soon as you know your new address. If you haven't moved yet, you may use your current address but should notify the council as soon as you have proof of your new address.

**My child has special educational needs - what should I do?**  
If your child has an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP), their place is allocated through a different process. Contact the SEN team at Devon County Council: 0345 155 1005

**Can I visit the school before applying?**  
Absolutely! We encourage all families to visit. See "School Tours" section above for dates and booking.

**Is there a breakfast or after-school club?**  
Yes, we offer wraparound care from 7:45am and until 5:30pm. More information will be provided at new parent meetings.

**What if my child turns 5 after September?**  
All children born between 1st September 2020 and 31st August 2021 start Reception in September 2025, regardless of when in that period their 5th birthday falls.

**Does attending the nursery next door guarantee a place?**  
No. Attendance at any nursery does not affect your application. You must still apply through the normal process.
