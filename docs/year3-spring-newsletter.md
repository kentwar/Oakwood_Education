# Year 3 Spring Term Newsletter

Dear Parents and Carers,

Welcome back! We hope you all had a wonderful winter break. Here's what Year 3 will be learning this term.

## Our Topic: Ancient Egypt

This term, we'll be travelling back in time to explore Ancient Egypt! The children will learn about pyramids, pharaohs, mummies, and daily life along the Nile.

### What we'll be learning:

**History**
- The importance of the River Nile
- How the pyramids were built
- Ancient Egyptian gods and beliefs
- Comparing ancient and modern Egypt

**Art & DT**
- Creating Egyptian death masks
- Designing and building our own pyramids
- Hieroglyphic writing and cartouche making

**English**
- Reading and writing myths and legends
- Non-fiction texts about Ancient Egypt
- Diary entries from the perspective of an Egyptian child
- Poetry inspired by the River Nile

**Maths**
- Multiplication and division (focus on 3, 4, and 8 times tables)
- Fractions
- Measurement (linking to pyramid dimensions)
- Problem-solving using Egyptian themes

**Science**
- Rocks and soils
- Forces and magnets
- Light and shadows

## How You Can Help at Home

- **Times Tables:** Please practise 3, 4, and 8 times tables regularly. We use Times Tables Rock Stars - ask your child to show you!

- **Egyptian Research:** Visit the library or look online together to learn more about Ancient Egypt
