## Attendance Matters

Well done to **Year 5** who had the best attendance this half-term at 97.8%!

**Whole school attendance this term: 96.1%**

A reminder that good attendance is crucial for your child's learning and progress. Please ensure your child is in school every day unless they are genuinely unwell.

If your child has been sick or had diarrhoea, please remember the **48-hour rule** - they must stay at home for 48 hours after the last episode to prevent spreading infection.
